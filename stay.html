stay with us

A handful of rooms above the studio.

We keep a handful of rooms above the studio. Guests become part of the rhythm of the place — waking into a working space, eating where the research happens, staying long enough to notice things. Every stay funds the lab, the labs fuel public programmes, and the programmes spark new collaborations.

the guesthouse

Where visitors fund creativity with every stay.

Rooms sit directly above the studio labs and café & co-working space, so guests are never far from the daily life of the place. Visitors are welcome to contribute their own vision — sit in on an open lab day, share a meal at Small Moments, or simply enjoy the garden.
